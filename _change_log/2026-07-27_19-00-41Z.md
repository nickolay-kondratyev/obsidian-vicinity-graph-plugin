The exclusion-enabled toggle and each sizing-metric toggle called `this.display()`, tearing down and rebuilding all six settings cards to reveal or disable a single dependent row. That discarded the user's scroll position and keyboard focus.

Both now mutate only the affected row: the sizing toggle flips its paired weight input's disabled state synchronously (before any await, so it paints the freshest value); the exclusion toggle creates/destroys just the patterns row's Setting subtree, re-reading enabled-ness from the store at paint time so an interleaved double-toggle cannot leave the row disagreeing with the checkbox. `applyReset()` deliberately keeps its full `display()` — a restore-defaults re-seed is a whole-tab concern.

New `e2e/settingsDependentRows.e2e.ts` asserts across each toggle that focus, scrollTop and an unrelated row's DOM node identity are undisturbed, plus `expect.poll` assertions that the writes still persist (verified non-vacuous by sabotage). Verified: check clean, 1053 unit tests, 83 e2e (1 pre-existing env-gated skip).
