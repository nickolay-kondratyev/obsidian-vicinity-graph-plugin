Motivated by two edge-routing tickets where a symptom reported from a real personal vault could not be reproduced under e2e: `ObsidianHarness` could only ever drive `.dev-vault`.

- `e2e/vaultTarget.ts` (new, pure) resolves the env var into a discriminated `VaultTarget`: `dev-vault-copy` (unchanged rm -rf + cpSync into `.tmp/e2e/vault`) or `external-in-place`. The destructive path is unreachable in external mode by construction, not by convention.
- External mode makes ZERO harness writes into the target vault. The plugin must already be installed and enabled there; otherwise the harness fails loudly with the exact symlink recipe. `extraFixtures` throws. `setEnable(true)` is retained (writes only localStorage in the sandbox user-data-dir, verified against the decompiled Obsidian 1.12.7 asar); `enablePluginAndSave`, which rewrites the vault's `community-plugins.json`, is never called.
- A per-spec `allowExternalVault: true` opt-in is required, so exporting the env var cannot silently aim destructive specs (e.g. the "Restore defaults" spec) at a real vault.
- `e2e/vaultTarget.test.ts` proves the safety claim: an allowlist source scan asserts every mutating `fs` destination in `e2e/*.ts` roots at `.tmp/e2e`, backed by a test enforcing the `import * as fs from "node:fs"` form the scan keys off. Both were mutation-tested with injected offenders. `vitest.config.ts` include widened to `e2e/**/*.test.ts`.
- `e2e/externalVault.e2e.ts` (skipped unless the var is set) opens `VICINITY_E2E_NOTE` and screenshots the graph into `.out/`.
- README documents the variable and states honestly what Obsidian itself still writes into whatever vault it opens.

With the var unset, behaviour is unchanged.
