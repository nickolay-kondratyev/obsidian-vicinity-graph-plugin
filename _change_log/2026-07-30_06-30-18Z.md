Follow-up to the settings-write failure-policy work, which made `UserNoticePort` THE user-visible message surface for the view layer. `src/view/ControlsActions.ts` still built `new Notice(...)` itself for "This note can't be pinned (no stable id).", so there were two ways to show a notice.

- `ControlsActions` takes a `UserNoticePort` as its last ctor param, threaded `main.ts` → `VicinityGraphView.tsx` exactly like the existing `ViewsRefreshPort`; its `obsidian` import is gone.
- `ControlsActions.test.ts` drops `vi.mock("obsidian", ...)` and now asserts the refusal copy through `FakeUserNotices`, paired with a negative case (a pin that lands says nothing) so the notice can neither be dropped nor become unconditional.
- `docs-internal/architecture-map.md` records the port's two producers and that `main.ts` is the only `Notice` construction site.
