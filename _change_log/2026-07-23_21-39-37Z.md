## What
Changed `BOUNDARY_PIN_SPECS` in `src/view/edgeRouting.ts` from 8 points (4 side-midpoints + 4 `dir:"all"` corners) to 12 outward-perpendicular pins: each of the 4 sides gets pins at fractions 0.25/0.5/0.75 (new `PIN_EDGE_Q1`/`PIN_EDGE_Q3` constants). The 4 corner pins are removed.

## Why
At a corner an edge could visually look like it continued *past* the node even though it terminated at the node. Side-only anchors remove that ambiguity.

## Scope
Folder-group boxes only. Note squares keep their single centre pin (perf-driven group-only decision from edge-routing__04 preserved). No routing cost-model change.

## Tests
Pure spec-lock test (12 entries, all outward, no corners) + real-WASM corner-clearance test (endpoints clear box corners by ~25px vs 0px under the old set). Full suite 681/681, tsc strict clean.
