## Why

Ticket nid_9wed7bqboqb83aghmt1sctv90_e: the dual-presenter ticket
(nid_armoson86j0ii8c33r1odo1rc_e) rewrote four Playwright specs but never ran the
gate, which needs a REAL Obsidian.

## What the gate found

The gate ran for real (pinned Obsidian 1.12.7, headless Ozone, no workarounds).
All four at-risk specs passed **unmodified** - the rewrites were correct, nothing
was stale, no assertion was tuned.

Verifying the flagged ~260px-panel risk surfaced a genuine, unguarded bug: the
controls panel body is a 60vh `overflow-y: auto` column flex, and its children
defaulted to `flex-shrink: 1`. So open disclosures **shrank** into the cap instead
of the body scrolling - each `overflow: hidden` section silently clipped its own
rows (Node sizing showed 138px of 336px) with no scrollbar and no error state.
Controls were unreachable.

## Fix

`.vicinity-graph-toolbar__body > * { flex-shrink: 0; }` in `src/view/graph-view.css`
- a no-op when content fits, and the body now genuinely scrolls (1086px content vs
a 479px cap). Guarded by a new test in `e2e/settingsUxVisual.e2e.ts` that opens every
top-level disclosure, compares each section's `clientHeight` to its `scrollHeight`
(1px named tolerance for dPR≠1 hosts), and asserts non-vacuity. Verified RED against
the pre-fix CSS both before and after the tolerance change.

Label wrapping on the longer panel labels was also measured with real geometry:
nothing clipped or ellipsised; three labels wrap to two lines cleanly.

## Result

`npm run test:e2e` 95 passed / 1 skipped (the pre-existing env-gated
`externalVault.e2e.ts`) / exit 0; `npm test` 1139 passed; `npm run check` clean.
Diff was 338 insertions / 0 deletions - no spec weakened, skipped or re-baselined.
