Closes ticket nid_fay1hu5sxcoygizopkkg0f0d7_e — the FINAL step of the settings-cleanup chain, and its measurement step.

## What changed

**Stage 1 — carry the kind.** `LinkKind = "link" | "embed"` is carried on OUTGOING references: markdown by `cache.embeds`/`cache.links` array provenance, frontmatter always `link`, canvas `type:"file"` nodes as embeds, and the `!` marker captured in the two shared regexes. Canvases are now ALWAYS parsed (option 3a) — the core-indexed outgoing shortcut and `CanvasCapability.ts` are gone, which also removes the boot-timing race from `resolvedLinks`. Incoming links stay kind-blind by owner scope cut, so no undocumented `getBacklinksForFile` surface was taken on.

**Stage 3 — the budget.** The traversal `Direction` axis generalises into a flat channel enum `outgoing-link | outgoing-embed | incoming` (3 kind-pure BFS runs per root); `Record<Channel, ...>` gives compile-time completeness for free.

## BREAKING — saved global depth settings reset

Global depth keys are renamed with NO migration (the plugin is unpublished, so stored-data changes take clean breaks):

| old | new | label |
|---|---|---|
| `outgoingDepth` | `linkDepthOut` | Links out |
| — | `embedDepthOut` (new) | Embeds out |
| `incomingDepth` | `linkDepthIn` | Links in |

Old keys are ignored and fall back to spec defaults. `PERSISTED_SHAPE_VERSION` is deliberately NOT bumped — unknown keys already fall back, so a bump would only widen the reset to every global. Announced in `docs-internal/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`.

## Two things worth knowing

1. **A settled decision's rationale was wrong.** The ticket claimed that at equal defaults the two outgoing channels union to exactly today's outgoing BFS. They do not: kind-pure results are the end-to-end-homogeneous paths, which coincide with the kind-blind walk only at depth <= 1, because changing kind takes two hops. Shipped defaults are 1/1/1 so there is zero observable change at ship, but the mixed-chain gap (`A ![[B]] -> B [[C]]` leaves C invisible) appears for the first user who raises an outgoing budget to 2 — equal budgets included. Kind-pure (6a) is still the shipped answer; per-kind hop accounting (6b) remains the expensive escape hatch if that gap proves user-visible.
2. **A disclosed behavior change.** For core-indexed canvases the rendered edge count now derives from our parse instead of core's `resolvedLinks` count. Kept on purpose (one authority per edge; re-reading `resolvedLinks` would re-expose the boot race) and pinned by a test where the two disagree.

## MEASUREMENT — did the settings cleanup pay off?

`embedDepthOut` is the first new settings field added under the declarative descriptor model. Honest cost: **25 files** = 6 production + 2 deliberate test tables + 17 fixture churn; **+91/-43 lines, ~14 lines of real code**. 4 of 6 production edits were compile-FORCED; exactly **1** hand-maintained step can still fail silently (spec leaf => declared row), now ticketed.

Against the pre-cleanup baseline of ~180 lines / ~15 files / ~8 silent parallel lists: **silent lists 8 -> 1, real code ~180 -> ~14**. Caveat stated plainly: roughly half that drop comes from the SEPARATE removal of the per-doc/per-central layer, not from the descriptor model. The descriptor model's own contribution is the 8->1 collapse of silent lists and the compile-forcing of most production edits. Verdict: it delivered.
