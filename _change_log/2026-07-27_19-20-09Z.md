## What changed

- `src/view/VicinityGraphSettingTab.ts` — new `nameToggle` helper beside `nameControl`; applied
  at the two `addToggle` sites (`renderExclusion`, `addSizingMetricRow`), so all 6 toggles are named
  and any future sizing metric inherits it.
- `e2e/settingsUxVisual.e2e.ts` — dropped the `:not([type=checkbox])` exemption, floor 20 -> 26,
  added three positive assertions (one of them `getByRole("checkbox", { name })`).

## The thing that was actually unknown

`node_modules/obsidian` ships types only, and `ToggleComponent` exposes just `toggleEl: HTMLElement`.
Verified against the RENDERED DOM on 1.12.7 via a throwaway marker-attribute probe (deleted):
`toggleEl` is the wrapping **`<label class="checkbox-container">`**, not the checkbox — and the ticket's
own premise of a `div` was wrong. That label is textless, which is precisely why the checkbox inherits
no name. So the label goes on `toggleEl.querySelector("input")`.

**WHY-NOT `aria-label` on `toggleEl` itself** — a `<label>` names its control by TEXT, not by
`aria-label`; adding text would change the pill's appearance.

**WHY-NOT throw when the input is missing** — a shifted Obsidian internal must not take the whole
settings tab down. The null branch is a documented no-op; the e2e guard is what fails loudly.

**WHY-NOT a vitest test** — unchanged from the slider effort: obsidian is types-only, so a real
`Setting` cannot be constructed and asserting a fake would test the fake.

**WHY-NOT `setTooltip`** — verified that Obsidian pops a tooltip for ANY `aria-label`, so it is
redundant.

## Naming

Exclusion toggle: `"Exclude notes from the graph"` (sole control in its row). Sizing toggles:
`` `${label} enabled` `` — the pre-existing two-controls-per-row rule already used by
`` `${label} weight` ``, and it avoids a strict-mode collision under Playwright's substring `getByLabel`.

## Caveat on "no visual change"

Resting render is identical. Hovering a toggle now shows Obsidian's tooltip, because any `aria-label`
earns one — the same behaviour sliders and number inputs already had since the slider a11y change.
