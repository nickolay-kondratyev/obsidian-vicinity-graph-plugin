New `src/view/settingsRowAccessors.ts` — a sibling to the pure-data `settingsRows.ts` (kept separate for SRP and to leave the node-side e2e import graph clean). One factory per control exposing `read(state)`, `bounds`, `settlesAt`, `interaction(value)`, plus `accept(raw)` where a user types.

- `VicinityGraphSettingTab.ts` and `SettingsRowView.tsx` no longer name an engine range table or a clamp; the tab's three slider builders collapsed to one.
- Duplicate `NODE_CAP_STEP` / `OUTLINE_DEPTH_SLIDER_STEP` / `DEPTH_SLIDER_STEP` declarations removed.
- `clampStepperDepth` (hard-wired to `linkDepthOut`) deleted; each depth row now clamps to its own spec leaf's bounds. `clampStepperDepth.test.ts` renamed to `settingsRowDepthClamp.test.ts` with all six assertions preserved.
- `SettingsTrackAccessor` demands a closed `max`, so a min-only control (node cap) at a slider is now a compile error instead of two divergent silent fallbacks.

No user-visible behavior, default, range, step or accessible name changed — verified by the reviewer against the write pipeline (sizing clamps are idempotent there; depth/outline were already clamped in both presenters before emitting).

Guards: new `settingsRowAccessors.test.ts` walks every row through a `switch` closed by `unhandledRowControl`; `settingsRowParity.test.ts` gained an `ACCESSOR_OWNED_SYMBOLS` scan over every row-rendering module. `settingsRowSpecCoverage` and `settingsProductDefaults` untouched.
