## What shipped

**(a) `setExclusive(false)` on the folder-group boundary pins** (`2d08ab1`, `9f92e77`).
libavoid derives a pin's exclusivity from its visibility directions, so the 12 directional pins on
a group box were each created EXCLUSIVE -- one connector apiece. Crowded sides exhausted the pool and
further edges fell back to the group CENTRE. Measured over 400 seeded scenes: non-facing attachments
82 -> 40 at realistic group degree (-2.3% route length), 24 -> 22 at low degree (-0.3%), routing time
flat. A deterministic 8-leaf probe goes from 5 of 8 edges on the wrong side to 8 of 8 facing.

**(b) `EDGE_ROUTING_SHAPE_BUFFER_PX` -> the `edgeRoutingClearancePx` setting** (`0703634`, `fc94c33`,
`dc71503`, `d323512`). Default 11 (swept, not assumed), clamp 6-14 step 1, surfaced as "Edge
clearance" in the Force layout card's Advanced spacing group. Dense `maxDetourRatio` 1.342 -> 1.244.
No `PERSISTED_SHAPE_VERSION` bump -- the parser defaults missing fields per field, so no stored
setting is lost; a bump would have discarded them all.

## Two premises the measurements overturned

1. **The corridor is sealed by the group's OWN member squares, not by neighbouring notes.**
   `ELK_GROUP_PADDING` insets members 16px and members are separate routing obstacles, so any buffer
   above that padding lets a member's clearance escape the group border and seal the group's own
   pins. Proven a mechanism rather than a curve fit: the cliff moves when the inset moves. The
   shipped 17px sat 1-2px OVER it -- the derivation `curvature / 2` had landed on the worst value.
2. **Smaller buffers REDUCE arrowhead overlap** (4.50% at 17px -> 3.24% at 5px), the opposite of what
   the ticket predicted. The old `buffer > arrowhead inset` invariant compared a perpendicular
   clearance against a longitudinal offset, so it never described a containment relation at all.

Both corrections were folded back into the ticket body and `docs-internal/research/`.

## Invariants replaced, not loosened

Asserted against the clamp RANGE, so they hold for every value a user can reach:
`buffer === EDGE_PAIR_CURVATURE_PX / 2` -> `max < GROUP_SIDE_PADDING_PX (16)`;
`buffer > EDGE_ARROWHEAD_INSET_MIN_PX` -> `min >= ARROWHEAD_HALF_WIDTH_PX (6)`.
Both bounds were extracted into real constants (one was trapped inside an elk syntax string) so they
are machine-checked instead of prose.

## Test-integrity work done along the way

- 11 real-wasm tests were PASSING when wasm was unavailable (`if (!loaded) return;`); they now skip.
- The route cache hashed only obstacles and edges, so the new slider would have served stale routes
  and looked dead. Caught RED-first.
- `setRoutingParameter(shapeBufferDistance, ...)` had no assertion at all -- hardcoding it back to 17
  left 779 unit tests and every e2e spec green. Now covered by a monotone route-length test.
- The facing-side property -- the entire point of the ticket -- had NO automated gate, because the
  `[eval]` detour ratios are byte-identical between the wrapping and non-wrapping arms. Now a
  committed e2e assertion on a new `facing` dev-vault fixture, which also replaced a manual
  real-vault screenshot check and revealed that item (a) alone already fixed the reported symptom.
