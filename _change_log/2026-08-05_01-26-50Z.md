Adversarial review of `a150312..HEAD` (content-fit node sizing, nid_cx5zoz7ptucg9nxalibv0mbjb_e). No functional defect found in the sizing code itself; these are the issues worth fixing.

1. **Dev-env reproducibility (fixed).** `scripts/setup-dev-vault.sh` seeds fixtures with `write_if_missing`, and `scripts/run-e2e.sh` runs against a COPY of that vault — so the content-fit change padding `facing-near*.md` with headings (to reach the 122px preview floor `e2e/edgeRouting.e2e.ts` is tuned around) never reached any vault that already existed. Added `write_fixture`: created when absent AND rewritten on drift, applied to the whole facing cluster. Verified by reverting one neighbour to its old one-line body, re-running `setup:dev-vault` (repaired, siblings untouched) and `test:e2e -- edgeRouting.e2e.ts` (green). Remaining spec-measured fixtures still on `write_if_missing` are ticketed: nid_v5510dvzp7nw9p4qrrpw7d35s_e.

2. **Doc drift (fixed).** `docs-internal/notes/settings.md` still pointed at the deleted `src/view/sizingMetrics.ts` and the old `Exclude<keyof SizingSpec, "metrics">`; `high-level-plan` still had minDepth driving "sizing decay"; `step-02-core-engine` described the removed metric system and the removed size-score chain level as current (marked SUPERSEDED, not rewritten — it is a step record); the step-06 smoke-run ticket listed a guard that no longer exists.

3. **README accuracy (fixed).** The Sizing bullet described the reveal floor as a thumbnail-only rule; it now covers the preview slot (122px) AND the attachment chip row (90px), which is what `NodeSizer.revealFloorPx` actually does.
