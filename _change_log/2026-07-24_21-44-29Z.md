Second settings-UX round (branch `settings`), scoped by the human to restore-defaults only.

- New pure `src/view/settingsResetPlan.ts`: `planSettingsReset(scope, ctx)` produces SettingsCommands from SETTINGS_SPEC/EngineDefaults, applied through the existing settings executor. Defaults keep a single source of truth; no literals duplicated in the view.
- Confirmation is data on the scope spec (`SettingsResetScopeSpec.confirmation`), and `requestReset()` is the single entry point for all six buttons.
- Tab-wide reset confirms via a new generic `src/view/ConfirmModal.ts` and states what is NOT reset (per-note depth overrides, pinned notes).
- Node-exclusion reset confirms whenever patterns exist and lists them verbatim, so hand-written regexes are visible even when the textarea is collapsed.
- Realigned four stale SETTINGS_SPEC baseline assertions and two doc comments with the intended `collidePaddingPx` (50 / max 100) and `linkGapPx` (max 250) bounds - human confirmed the earlier bump was deliberate.

Verified against a real Obsidian: settings e2e 11/11 + 8/8 + 7/7, `npm test` 769/769, `npm run check` clean.

Deferred to tickets: input debouncing + bounds/regex validation, avoiding full display() rebuilds, and the orphan `groupByFolder`/`edgeVisibility` settings.
