Adversarial review of e507fd2..HEAD. No behavior change in the layout decision itself (npm test 1576 passed, npm run check, nodeResize.e2e.ts 8 passed).

1. GraphStructureDiff hand-compared widthPx/heightPx inline; a third field on NodeSizeOverridePx would have been silently ignored, leaving that dimension unable to trigger a relayout. Equality now lives beside nodeSizeOverridePx in src/view/graphIdentity.ts, with the field list derived from Record<keyof NodeSizeOverridePx, true> (same idiom as FORCE_LAYOUT_FIELDS). 6 BDD cases in graphIdentity.test.ts.
2. README's new 'graph re-arranges itself on release' bullet did not mention that the relayout also refits the viewport — a user-visible side effect known only to ticket nid_ct22qotgtw4rezbdn5m0diyb3_e. Added as a Known caveat; the ticket says the caveat is interim and does not pre-empt its decision.
