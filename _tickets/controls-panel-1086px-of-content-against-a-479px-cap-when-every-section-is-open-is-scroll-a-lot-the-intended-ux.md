The in-graph controls panel is a ~260px-wide surface whose body is capped at 60vh
(measured 479px in the e2e host). With every top-level disclosure open, its content is
1086px - i.e. the panel scrolls roughly 2.3 screens.

This is now CORRECT behaviour: ticket nid_9wed7bqboqb83aghmt1sctv90_e fixed a real bug
where the body's flex children defaulted to `flex-shrink: 1`, so open sections silently
SHRANK into the cap and clipped their own rows with no scrollbar and no error state
(fix: `.vicinity-graph-toolbar__body > * { flex-shrink: 0 }` in src/view/graph-view.css,
guarded by the clip test in e2e/settingsUxVisual.e2e.ts). The panel now scrolls properly.

WHAT IS UNDECIDED (needs the owner): is the panel MEANT to be used with several sections
open at once? If yes, 2.3 screens of scroll on a 260px surface is poor UX and the answer
might be a wider or taller panel, an accordion (only one section open at a time), or
moving the long sections to the settings tab. If the panel is meant to be used one section
at a time, no change is needed and this ticket closes as WONTFIX.

No code change should be made until that decision is made - hence the `decide` tag.
